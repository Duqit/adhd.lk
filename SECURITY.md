# Security policy

ADHD.lk stores personal data for account holders, including journal entries and mood check-ins,
so we take security reports seriously.

## Reporting a vulnerability

**Please do not open a public issue.** Report privately in one of these ways:

1. **GitHub:** use [Report a vulnerability](https://github.com/duqit/adhd.lk/security/advisories/new)
   on this repository's Security tab.
2. **Email:** [support@adhd.lk](mailto:support@adhd.lk), with "Security" in the subject.

Please include:

- what you found and where (URL or file)
- steps to reproduce
- what an attacker could do with it

We will acknowledge your report within a few working days, keep you updated, and credit you when
it is fixed, unless you prefer not to be named.

## Scope

- **In scope:** the code in this repository and the live site at https://adhd.lk.
- **Out of scope:**
  - denial-of-service and volume testing
  - automated scanning that floods the site
  - social engineering
  - reports that only concern missing best-practice headers without a demonstrated impact

When testing, **only use accounts you created yourself**, and never access or change other
people's data. Testing within these rules in good faith will not lead to legal action from us.
